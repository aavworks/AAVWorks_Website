OUR SPECIALISED SERVICES

Complete Solutions Across
The Railway Lifecycle

From preliminary feasibility and circuit drafting to SIL-4 safety documentation and station commissioning, AAV Works delivers certified railway signalling and interlocking services for modern rail networks.

CORE DISCIPLINE 01

Railway Signalling & Circuit Engineering

Our proficient engineering team specializes in crafting robust, fail-safe circuits that serve as the bedrock of Railway Signalling infrastructure. We excel across all major modern signalling technologies:

Electronic Interlocking (EI) Design
Scheme plans, Selection tables (Table of Control), VDU Control Layouts, Application Logic, and Interface Circuits.
Automatic Train Protection (ATP) & Kavach / TCAS
Kavach / TCAS Designing, ETCS Level 1 & 2 Designing, PTC, and Communication-Based Train Control (CBTC).
Independent Verification (Proof Consultancy)
Licensed IRSE / IRSTELO consultants who have completed 100+ Independent Verification activities across Indian Railways.
CORE DISCIPLINE 02

Turnkey Projects & Station Commissioning

Our dedicated project engineers have successfully installed and commissioned more than 350+ stations across India, covering diverse OEM technologies including Medha, Kyosan, Ansaldo/Hitachi, and Siemens.

Installation & Erection
Track circuits, axle counters, point machines, and multi-aspect signals.
Site Acceptance Testing (SAT)
Rigorous functional testing, square sheets, and interlocking cross-verification.
Safety Audits & Risk Mgmt
Zero-incident safety culture protecting personnel and rail assets.
Regulatory Documentation
CRS sanction dossiers and complete as-built documentation.
CORE DISCIPLINE 03

System Assurance & RAMS Management

RAMS (Reliability, Availability, Maintainability, Safety) management in Railway Systems stands at the very center of our technical assurance services, complying strictly with international CENELEC standards.

CENELEC Standards Compliance
EN 50126 (RAMS lifecycle), EN 50128 (Software for railway control), and EN 50129 (Safety electronic systems).
Hazard Log & Safety Cases
FMECA, fault tree analysis, preliminary hazard analysis, and SIL-4 Safety Case preparation.
Proven Global MNC Engagements
PSD SIL 2 Documentation for Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL) and Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor (EDFC) documentation for Siemens.
CORE DISCIPLINE 04

Rail Software & Signalling Automation

We build custom software tools that eliminate tedious, error-prone manual drafting tasks. From automated Boolean logic generation to interactive trainee traffic simulators, our software enhances rail efficiency and zero-defect deployments.

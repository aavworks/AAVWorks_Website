## Primary Audience
1. **Indian Railways & Zonal Authorities**: Chief Signalling & Telecom Engineers (CSTE), Dy. CSTEs, and Divisional Officers seeking verified S&T drawings, Yard layouts, Selection tables, and ABS commissioning support.
2. **EPC Contractors & Rail Infrastructure Giants**: L&T, Tata Projects, RVNL, IRCON, KEC, Siemens, Alstom looking for reliable design proof consultants, circuit designers, and FAT/SAT testing partners.
3. **Railway Professionals & Signalling Engineers**: Experienced IRSE/IRSTELO certified engineers and aspiring rail talent seeking high-impact careers in modern railway automation.

## Core Offerings & Solutions
- **Engineering**: Circuit Designing, Scheme Plans, Selection Tables (TOC), Application Logic, Interface Circuits, Independent Verification (Proof Consultancy) by certified IRSE/IRSTELO license holders, FAT (IFAT/CFAT) and SAT testing.
- **Projects**: End-to-end Railway Signalling EPC, Electronic Interlocking (EI) installations, Cable Route Plans, Outdoor Signalling Gear erection, Station Commissioning (350+ stations benchmark).
- **System Assurance (RAMS)**: Reliability, Availability, Maintainability, and Safety analysis complying with CENELEC standards (EN 50126, EN 50128, EN 50129), Hazard Logs, SIL-4 Safety Case preparation, OMPD documentation.
- **Software Services**: Automated interlocking logic generation, station traffic simulators, trackside cable tracking systems, and digital twin test rigs.

## Proprietary Software Products
1. **AVW-SENSIO**: High-Density Interlocking I/O Unit (200ms cycle time, 448 IO channels, multi-cabin Ethernet networking, 10A output card).
2. **AVW-SENTRIS**: Automatic Factory Acceptance Testing (A-FAT) Interlocking Validator (adaptable to all 18 Indian Railway zonal rules).
3. **AVW Traffic Simulator**: Station Master & Dispatcher train movement simulator with real-time fault injection and trainee assessment.
4. **AVW Cable Tracker**: Field GIS trackside cable identification, route mapping, and core diagnostic application.
5. **AVW Algen & L2L**: Algorithmic logic and circuit generation engine delivering zero-error railway schematics.

- **Platform**: Web (Responsive, Desktop 1920px to Mobile 360px)
- **Architecture**: Modern Static Multi-Page Web Application with modular ES6 JavaScript, Vite build toolchain, custom SVG icons, and Tailwind utility framework.
- **Deploy Target**: GitHub Pages / Static hosting (Netlify / Vercel compatible).

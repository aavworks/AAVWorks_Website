# Product documents (PDF drop-in folder)

Each card on the Products page links to a PDF in `public/documents/<folder>/<file>.pdf`.
Drop a PDF in with the **exact file name** below and its card switches from
**PDF coming soon** to **View PDF** automatically (see `src/js/documents.js`).
Optional: add a preview image at `public/documents/previews/<file>.jpg` (720 px wide) and
use the `has-preview` card markup in `products.html` to show it instead of the paper icon.

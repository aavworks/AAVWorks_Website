OUR STORY & ETHOS

Driven by Engineering.
Guided by Purpose.

Established to advance modern railway infrastructure, AAV WORKS was founded by a dynamic, result-oriented group of railway engineering professionals. Our mission is to transform the railway signaling landscape by blending proven engineering practices with advanced technologies to deliver fail-safe, standard-compliant design solutions. We specialize in managing railway projects across every milestone, guiding design packages from concept to commissioning with an uncompromising focus on safety, reliability, and timely delivery.

Our core expertise spans conventional yard signaling, Automatic Block Signaling (ABS), modern Electronic Interlocking (EI), and advanced Automatic Train Protection (ATP) systems such as Kavach. Driven by in-depth domain knowledge and strict adherence to RDSO standards, statutory safety directives, and Indian Railway Signal Engineering Manual (IRSEM) guidelines, our team delivers high-precision drawing packages, technical calculations, and interface documentation.

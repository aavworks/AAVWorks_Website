Driven by Engineering.

Established to advance modern railway infrastructure, AAV WORKS was founded by a dynamic, result-oriented group of railway engineering professionals. We blend proven engineering practices with advanced technologies to deliver fail-safe, standard-compliant railway signaling design solutions — from concept to commissioning, with an uncompromising focus on safety, reliability, and timely delivery.
